One line of out-of-office auto-reply: I'm out this week teaching my parrot to say "per my last email" — and your message will be answered by whichever of us gets to it first. It reads like a real out-of-office note until the last few words quietly hand your email to the bird. No beach, no "limited access to email", no "will respond on my return": the joke is one vivid scene, a specific phrase every office worker recognises, and a twist that lands in the final clause.
